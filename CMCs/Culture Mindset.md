 Growing through the challenge

- Growth Mindset - The belief that our abilities can be developed 
		   through learning, effort, practice, feedback, and persistence.
			
			-how do my brain change 
			-how do I grow
			-what am I saying

- Fixed Mindset - The belief that our abilities are fixed and that success 
		  depends primarily on natural talent or inteligence.


Your success in the Academy will bot be determined by what you kner on Day One.
It will be influenced by waht you believe about your ability to learn.
