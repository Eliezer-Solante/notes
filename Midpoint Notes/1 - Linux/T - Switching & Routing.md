## 📖 What is Switching in Networking?

- **Definition** Switching is the process of forwarding data packets between devices within the same network (usually a LAN) using a **network switch**.
    
- **Purpose** Ensures efficient communication by directing data only to the intended recipient device, rather than broadcasting to all devices.
    
- **Device** A **switch** is a hardware device that connects multiple computers, printers, servers, etc., within a local area network (LAN).

## 📖 What is Routing?

- **Definition** Routing is the process of selecting paths for data packets to travel across **different networks**.
- **Purpose** Ensures that data can move from a source device in one network to a destination device in another network.
- **Device** A **router** is the hardware that performs routing, connecting multiple networks (e.g., your home LAN to the Internet).
